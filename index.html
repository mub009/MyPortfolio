About me

I studied Computer Applications at the University of Calicut and started as a junior engineer in Kozhikode. Since 2019 I have worked as a senior software engineer for a group of medical complexes in Saudi Arabia, where the software has to be right because clinics, insurers and the tax authority all depend on it.

That job taught me to care about the unglamorous parts: a query that stays fast when the table grows, a deployment that does not break at midnight, an integration that still works after the other side changes its API.

Alongside it I take freelance projects. Clients usually come to me with one of three things: something new to build, something broken to fix, or a server nobody wants to touch.

How I work. I agree scope, timeline and cost before I start. You get regular updates and straight answers. The code lives in Git, comes with notes, and I stay around after delivery for fixes.
