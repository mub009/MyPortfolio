About me

I studied Computer Applications at the University of Calicut and started as a junior engineer in Kozhikode. Since 2019 I have been a senior software engineer for a group of medical complexes in Saudi Arabia, where clinics, insurers and the tax authority all depend on the software being right.

That job taught me to care about the quiet parts: a query that stays fast when the table grows, a deployment that does not break at midnight, an integration that still works after the other side changes its API.

On freelance projects I agree scope, timeline and cost before I start. You get regular updates and straight answers, the code lives in Git, and I stay around after delivery for fixes.
